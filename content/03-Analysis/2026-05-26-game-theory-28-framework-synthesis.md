## The Three Convergent Explanations

The US attacked Iran to knock out 20% of world energy supply → forcing Europe/China/Japan to buy US treasuries to finance the $39 trillion debt. Secondary goal: gain control of four key trade choke points (Hormuz, Gibraltar, Malacca, Cape of Good Hope). This directly supports [[CLAIM-001-us-iran-war]] and [[CLAIM-002-strait-of-hormuz]].

### 2. Eschatology: The Religious Trigger
Religious factions in the US believe the Iran war triggers: destruction of Al-Aqsa → Third Temple → War of Gog and Magog → Antichrist → Messiah/Second Coming. The critical new insight: **all four combatants** (US, Israel, Iran, Russia) share similar eschatological frameworks because all four religions trace back to the same Levantine conflict pattern. This generates new [[CLAIM-021-eschatological-alignment-four-powers]].

### 3. Imperial Decline: Elite Overproduction (Turchin)
Peter Turchin's elite overproduction theory: too many elites competing for zero-sum power → civil war → projected outward as foreign aggression. Plus financialization (debt slavery) and demographic crisis (aging + immigration + ethnic tension). This explains US aggression in Iran, Venezuela, Greenland, Canada, Mexico simultaneously. Supports [[CLAIM-003-national-draft-civil-war]] and [[CLAIM-015-depopulation-resource-wars]].

**Why they converge**: All three explanations predict the same outcomes — expanding Middle East war, Third Temple construction attempt, messianic figure emergence, and eventual US imperial collapse.

The most significant new material:

**Money as existing "god"**: Banks print money from nothing (alchemy: lead into gold). Money is God because it is omnipresent. But banks must also destroy money to maintain scarcity → mechanism: (a) engineered recession or (b) wars. **The 2025-2026 Middle East wars are destroying the excess money from 10-20 years of QE and pandemic stimulus.** New claim: [[CLAIM-019-wars-monetary-destruction]].

For AI to replace money as god it needs: (1) omniscience through combining all world databases, (2) religious status by making people believe it is conscious/divine. **The civil war**: transnational capital (money-god = capitalism, freedom) vs. AI-god faction (techno-Marxism, techno-slavery). Trump and nationalism serve as transition agents dismantling old money-god institutions to enable new AI-god infrastructure. Deepens [[CLAIM-008-trump-technate]] significantly.
