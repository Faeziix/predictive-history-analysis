# US Automatic Military Draft Registration by December 2026

## Summary
American men ages 18-26 will be automatically registered for the military draft starting December 2026 under a measure in the annual defense policy bill, signed into law before the Iran war with bipartisan support. No actual draft has been authorized — Congress would need to pass additional legislation. Trump cannot activate a draft via executive action alone.

## Key Data Points
- Automatic registration: December 2026, men ages 18-26
- Legislative vehicle: annual National Defense Authorization Act
- Passed: bipartisan support, before Iran war
- Actual draft: requires Congressional authorization of the Military Selective Service Act amendment
- Trump alone: cannot activate draft
- War Resisters' International: coalition calling to abolish Selective Service System
- Context: war has renewed public attention on draft infrastructure
