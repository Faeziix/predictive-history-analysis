# US-Iran Ceasefire on Life Support; Deal "Largely Negotiated"

## Summary
April 8 ceasefire between US and Iran remains in place but "on life support" per Trump, with intermittent fighting since May 4. Trump stated May 23 that a peace deal reopening the Strait of Hormuz is "largely negotiated" — then walked back the claim the next day. Key sticking points: timing of sanctions relief, Iran's nuclear program, frozen Iranian assets, and how the Lebanon front is addressed.

## Key Data Points
- Ceasefire: April 8, 2026; intermittent fighting resumed May 4
- Trump: deal "largely negotiated" (May 23) then retracted (May 24)
- MOU under negotiation — would reopen Strait of Hormuz
- Lebanon coverage: Iranian demand that MOU address "all fronts including Lebanon"
- US struck Iran as recently as May 25 (NPR)
- Operation Project Freedom (May 4) to escort ships out — paused May 6 due to "great progress"

## Bias Assessment
CNBC and NPR are mainstream US outlets. Coverage balanced; Trump quotes directly cited. Administration framing dominates but dissenting analysis included.
