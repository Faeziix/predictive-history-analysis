# Over 36,500 Killed in Iran's Deadliest Massacre, Documents Reveal

## Summary
Iran International's report claiming over 36,500 protesters killed in the 8–9 January 2026 crackdown — an escalation from its earlier 12,000 figure (13 January) — attributed to classified documents the outlet reviewed and to anonymous sources inside Iran's security institutions. The report itself concedes that independent verification is impossible under the communications blockade. Anchor source for the [[casualty-inflation-narrative]] note and the [[2026-01-25-iran-international-36500-claim]] evidence note.

## Key Data Points
- Claimed figure: over 36,500 killed (escalated 12,000 → 27,500 → 30,000 → 33,000 → 36,500 across ~10 days)
- Sources: anonymous SNSC-adjacent source, two presidential-office sources, IRGC members; eyewitnesses; medical staff; unpublished classified documents
- Outlet's own caveat: "confirming a final figure will require further, detailed documentation"; "independent verification remains impossible"
- Roughly 5× the contemporaneous [[hrana-methodology|HRANA verified count]] (~7,000)

## Bias Assessment
Credibility rated **medium**, not high, for this specific claim: Iran International is an established outlet but openly oppositional, and the 36,500 figure is self-sourced to anonymous insiders and to documents that were never published — by the outlet's own admission unverifiable. It is reliable as a record of *what Iran International reported*, not as a verified casualty count. This source is the primary instance of the [[manufacturing-consent-mechanism]] examined in this cluster.
