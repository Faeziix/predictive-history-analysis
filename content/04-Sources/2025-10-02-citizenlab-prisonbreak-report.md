# We Say You Want a Revolution: PRISONBREAK — An AI-Enabled Influence Operation Aimed at Overthrowing the Iranian Regime

## Summary
The **primary forensic source** behind the Pahlavi influence-operation story. Citizen Lab Report No. 189 (2 October 2025) documents **PRISONBREAK**, a coordinated network of **50+ inauthentic X accounts** running an AI-enabled influence operation that incited Iranians to revolt and promoted regime change, assessed as **most likely run by an Israeli government entity or a private subcontractor working closely with it**. This is the load-bearing, methodology-driven research that the Haaretz/TheMarker investigation and downstream press coverage rest on. Anchor source for [[israeli-influence-operation-iran]] and the [[2025-10-03-haaretz-citizenlab-pahlavi-influence-op]] evidence note.

**Authors:** Alberto Fittarelli, Ron Deibert, Marcus Michaelsen, Maia Scott, with Darren Linvill (Clemson University).

## Key Data Points (forensic)
- **Network size:** 50+ inauthentic X profiles (53 analyzed); 30 of 53 used obscured-face / stolen profile photos from stock sites or Pinterest.
- **Dormancy → activation:** accounts created Feb–Dec 2023, but **~99.5% of posting happened after January 2025**.
- **Timezone signal:** activity ramped at 6:00 UTC, peaked 7:00 UTC, wound down after 15:00 UTC — a regular work day in **UTC+3 (Israel/Tehran)**, not organic global user behavior.
- **Client signal:** ≥75% of posts via X's **desktop web app** — anomalous for a mobile-first platform.
- **Registration signal:** ~half the accounts registered via the same obscure "b…" email domain.
- **Coordination markers:** identical hashtag strings, shared content, same posting hours, coordination with the pro-Israel `@TelAviv_Tehran` persona.

## AI-Generated Content Documented
- **Evin Prison deepfake** (23 June 2025, 12:05 Tehran time) — posted *while airstrikes were ongoing*; calls to storm the prison within ~90 min. Researchers say the timing "strongly suggests prior knowledge of the Israeli military action."
- **Fake BBC Persian** broadcasts/articles (officials fleeing Iran) — BBC confirmed it never published them.
- **"8 o'clock cry" protest videos** (≥9 between 20–22 June, 20k–60k views each) with "Death to Khamenei" chants.
- **Deepfake of musicians** Toomaj Salehi, Mehdi Yarrahi, Shervin Hajipour (13 Aug 2025) singing altered "Baraye" lyrics — visible AI artifacts (backwards walking, duplicated tattoos).
- **Water-crisis propaganda** (Khamenei "stealing water from a baby"; bomb-timer imagery).

## Attribution & Confidence
- Assessment: **high likelihood** an Israeli government entity or close contractor ran it. US-involvement hypotheses rated **medium-high / low**.
- Corroborating signal: Israeli FM **Gideon Saar reposted** the Evin Prison deepfake.
- **Stated limitation (verbatim caveat):** "At present, we cannot conclusively attribute this to a particular entity" — platform access restrictions deny researchers the metadata for definitive attribution.
