# The Israeli Influence Operation in Iran Pushing to Reinstate the Shah Monarchy

## Summary
A joint Haaretz / TheMarker investigation, corroborated by independent forensic research from the University of Toronto's Citizen Lab, revealing an Israeli-funded, AI-driven, Persian-language influence operation that used fake personas and avatars posing as Iranian citizens to promote Reza Pahlavi and the restoration of the monarchy, synchronized with Israeli military actions. Anchor source for the [[israeli-influence-operation-iran]] note and the [[2025-10-03-haaretz-citizenlab-pahlavi-influence-op]] evidence note. Original is paywalled; full-text mirror: <https://theiranpost.com/the-israeli-influence-operation-in-iran-pushing-to-reinstate-the-shah-monarchy/>.

## Key Data Points
- Operation run out of Israel, funded by a private entity that receives Israeli government support
- Used fake accounts and AI-generated "avatars" on X and Instagram posing as Iranian citizens
- Forensic backbone is [[2025-10-02-citizenlab-prisonbreak-report|Citizen Lab Report No. 189 "PRISONBREAK"]]: 50+ inauthentic X accounts, dormant from 2023, ~99.5% posting after Jan 2025
- Citizen Lab assessed it "most likely run by an Israeli government entity or a close contractor" (high likelihood; not conclusively attributable)
- Synchronized with Israeli strikes (e.g., Evin Prison) and the June 12-day war
- Co-opted authentic hashtags ("Death to Khamenei"); produced an AI video of Netanyahu and Pahlavi in a "Free Tehran"

## Bias Assessment
Notably, this is the *Israeli* press (Haaretz/TheMarker) reporting against the Israeli state's own influence apparatus, which reduces the likelihood of anti-Israel slant in the core finding; the independent Citizen Lab forensic corroboration is the load-bearing element. Discipline caveat: the investigation documents a Pahlavi-promotion amplification op — it does not, by itself, establish that the [[casualty-inflation-narrative|36,500 casualty figure]] was a product of the same operation.
