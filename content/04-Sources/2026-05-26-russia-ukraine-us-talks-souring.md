# Ukraine and Russia Both Sour on US Peace Negotiations

## Summary
Both Ukraine and Russia have expressed growing disillusionment with Trump-led mediation. Putin proposed Gerhard Schröder as envoy (rejected by Europe). Russia insists no ceasefire without comprehensive territorial settlement. Ukraine pivoting toward European-led mediation track. War continues with no resolution.

## Key Data Points
- Russia demand: Ukraine must cede territory before any ceasefire
- Putin: no NATO troops on Ukrainian soil
- European alternative: Angela Merkel and Mario Draghi mooted as possible envoys
- Putin counter-proposal: Gerhard Schröder (rejected by EU leaders)
- Negotiation tracks: military, political, economic, territorial, security guarantees — all inter-dependent
- Trump: told Zelensky Ukraine doesn't "have the cards"
- European involvement: UK/France discussing "military hubs" inside Ukraine
