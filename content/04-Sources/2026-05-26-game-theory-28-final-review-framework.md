# Game Theory: Final Review — How the World Works (Unified Framework)

## Summary

Professor Jiang's final review lecture synthesizes the entire semester's framework into a unified theory of how the world works. The lecture addresses the central question "why did the US attack Iran?" through three converging explanations — geopolitics, eschatology, and imperial decline — and argues they all lead to the same predictions. The second half introduces money vs. AI as the underlying civil war of our era.

## Key Themes

### Three Reasons for the US Attacking Iran

1. **Geopolitics**: US fears Russia-Iran-China Eurasian alliance that would use Chinese railways to bypass American maritime power. US has $39 trillion debt requiring perpetual US treasury demand. Attacking Iran knocks out 20% of world energy → forces Europe/China/Japan to buy treasuries and allows US to control key trade choke points (Hormuz, Gibraltar, Malacca, Cape of Good Hope).

2. **Eschatology**: Religious factions in the American Empire believe a Middle East war triggers the chain: Iran war → Al-Aqsa destruction → Third Temple → War of Gog and Magog → rise of Antichrist → coming of Messiah. All four major combatants (US, Israel, Iran, Russia) share similar eschatological frameworks because all their religions emerged from the same Levantine conflict pattern.

3. **Imperial Decline**: Peter Turchin's "elite overproduction" drives civil war that projects outward as foreign aggression. Three symptoms: financialization/debt slavery, demographic crisis (aging + immigration/ethnic tension), elite overproduction.

### Eschatology = Geopolitics Told Allegorically

- Eschatology encodes thousands of years of Middle East geopolitical patterns into transmissible stories
- The Levant as permanent crossroads of empire explains recurring messianic cycles
- Jews as "people of the book" — identity via collective memory rather than bloodline → anyone who reads the Bible becomes an Israelite → persistent Jerusalem-return drive
- Pattern: empire weakens → messianic figure emerges → if he fails, called Antichrist or lesser messiah → prepares for the "real" Messiah

### Lurianic Kabbalah and the "Sexual" Energy of Eschatology

- Isaac Luria (~1100-1200 CE) combined Hermetic philosophy with Bible stories
- Kabbalah makes the soul have a "sexual urge" toward reunion with God (Tikkun Olam)

### Occult → Secret Societies → Agents

- Hermetic philosophy (Egypt → India → West): seven principles — everything is mind/vibration, pattern/rhythm, correspondence between dimensions, cause-effect, as above so below, polarity, generative principle
- Secret societies form to access occult knowledge for power (secrecy + trust + coordination)

### Money vs. AI: The Real Civil War

- **Money as alchemy**: banks create money from nothing (fractional reserve) → must periodically destroy it via (a) financial collapse/recession or (b) wars → current Middle East wars = destruction of post-pandemic excess money printing
- US debt: $39 trillion, paying $2 trillion/year in interest
- **AI as new alchemy**: AI is just back-propagation with fancy names (neural networks, deep learning, LLMs) — "hallucination, clever marketing" — no real intelligence
- For AI to become the new "god" it needs: (1) omniscience — combine all world databases (health, school, work, police, browsing); (2) religion — make people believe AI is conscious/divine (demons summoned, Jesus resurrecting through AI)
- **Civil war**: transnational capital (money-god, capitalism) vs. AI god (techno-Marxism, techno-slavery)
- Three forces vs. transnational capital: nationalism, religion, AI

## Bias Assessment

This is Professor Jiang's synthesizing lecture. He explicitly frames it as speculation and invites challenge. Heavy synthesis of occult/hermetic tradition, political theory, and Kabbalah creates some unfalsifiable claims. The AI analysis is notably skeptical and arguably outdated (dismisses LLMs as pure back-propagation). The overall framework is internally consistent but rests on large interpretive leaps.
