# US–Iran Sign Memorandum of Understanding to End 2026 War (Geneva, June 19)

## Summary
After nearly four months of war (begun 28 February 2026 with US–Israeli airstrikes that killed Supreme Leader Khamenei), the US and Iran finalized a memorandum of understanding to end hostilities, with a ceremonial signing scheduled for 19 June 2026 in Geneva. The deal establishes a 60-day ceasefire and — critically — ends the war via negotiated settlement rather than the decisive US ground-invasion defeat that had been predicted.

## Key Data Points
- War duration: 28 Feb 2026 – June 2026 (~4 months), fought as an air/naval war; no US ground invasion occurred
- MOU announced 14 June (PM Sharif of Pakistan as mediator); signing 19 June, Geneva
- Terms: cessation of hostilities in Lebanon; end of Iranian restrictions on Strait of Hormuz; drawdown of US military assets from region; partial sanctions relief; release of some frozen Iranian assets
- Trump announced immediate lifting of US naval blockade of ships docked at Iranian ports
- Earlier ceasefire (April 7–8) had collapsed; this is the second, more comprehensive attempt
- Iran's regime survived; negotiating from a position of demands (Hormuz + Lebanon coverage)
