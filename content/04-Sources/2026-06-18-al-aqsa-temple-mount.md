# Al-Aqsa Sealed Off During War; Temple Mount Movement Gains Ground

## Summary
During the Iran war Israel sealed off Al-Aqsa in what reporting called an "unprecedented" lockdown. In January 2026 Netanyahu backed Ben-Gvir's policy expanding Jewish prayer at the Temple Mount compound (a status-quo shift). ~70,000 religious-nationalist participants joined the May 2026 Jerusalem Day Flag March, with Temple Mount imagery and "This is not Al-Aqsa, it's the Temple Mount" signage. A congressional initiative to recognize Israeli sovereignty over Al-Aqsa has been floated. The mosque has not been destroyed, but the Temple-movement trajectory is intensifying.

## Key Data Points
- Al-Aqsa placed under lockdown during the war ("unprecedented move")
- Jan 2026: Netanyahu backs expanded Jewish prayer at Temple Mount (Ben-Gvir policy)
- May 14, 2026: ~70,000 at Jerusalem Day Flag March; Temple Mount imagery prominent
- Israel Police recruiting far-right/religious activists to staff Al-Aqsa compound (June 2026)
- US congressional initiative to recognize Israeli sovereignty over Al-Aqsa (raised Oct 2025+)
- No destruction of the mosque has occurred — endpoint of the prediction remains unrealized
