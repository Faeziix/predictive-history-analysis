# Game Theory #26: The Holy Empire of AI

## Summary

The most philosophical episode in the Game Theory series. Jiang argues that popular conspiracy theories (Knights Templar → Freemasons → Illuminati → one-world government in Jerusalem) are *metaphorically true* but *literally false*: they are not factual, but they are predictive and explanatory. The "literal" version is that throughout history, elites obsessed with power, order, and control have converged in secret societies not through a 700-year conspiracy but because power-seeking people behave identically across time. The Freemason eschatological goal — a "holy empire of reason" created through world wars and culminating in an AI surveillance state — is being fulfilled by Sam Altman (OpenAI), Peter Thiel (Palantir), Larry Ellison (Oracle), and Elon Musk, not because they read Albert Pike but because they think like him. Oracle was created by the CIA/Navy. Palantir is the ultimate AI surveillance company. Operation Stargate ($500B data centers) is the infrastructure for this state. Brzezinski predicted all of it in 1970.
