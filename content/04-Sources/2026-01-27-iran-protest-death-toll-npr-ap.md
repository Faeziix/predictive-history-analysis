# At Least 6,126 Killed in Iran Crackdown, Rights Group Says

## Summary
NPR, carrying Associated Press reporting, documents HRANA's confirmed protest death toll of at least 6,126 as of 27 January 2026, alongside the explicit caveat that the AP could not independently verify the toll because Iranian authorities cut the internet and disrupted calls. The report describes HRANA's track record of accuracy and its on-the-ground verification network. This is the anchor source for the [[hrana-methodology]] note and the [[2026-01-27-hrana-7000-verified-toll]] evidence note.

## Key Data Points
- HRANA confirmed at least 6,126 killed as of 27 Jan 2026 (5,777 protesters, 214 government forces, 86 children, 49 civilians)
- 41,800+ arrests reported
- AP "unable to independently assess the death toll" due to the internet/communications cutoff
- HRANA "has been accurate in multiple rounds of unrest" and verifies each death via an activist network

## Bias Assessment
NPR/AP is high-credibility wire-grade reporting with explicit sourcing and explicit uncertainty disclosure. The figure is attributed to HRANA, whose method is self-described and not externally audited, but is corroborated across multiple independent outlets. The report models the epistemic discipline this cluster aims to preserve: it states what is verified, by whom, and what cannot be confirmed.
