# 2026 Global Food Crisis: 318M in Crisis Hunger, Fertilizer Up ~80%

## Summary
The Strait of Hormuz disruption has propagated from energy into agriculture exactly along the oil→fertilizer→food chain. The UN/FAO warns of a severe global food crisis within 6–12 months; 318 million people are in crisis-level hunger; Gaza and Sudan are in confirmed famine simultaneously — the first time this century. Fertilizer prices are up ~80% year-on-year.

## Key Data Points
- Hormuz carried ~30% of global fertilizer exports plus sulfur (essential for phosphate fertilizer)
- Fertilizer prices +~80% YoY following the Hormuz crisis
- 318M people in crisis-level hunger; 16 FAO/WFP hunger hotspots; 6 at famine-risk: Sudan, Gaza, South Sudan, Yemen, Mali, Haiti
- Gaza and Sudan in confirmed famine simultaneously (first this century)
- UK food inflation forecast 9–10% by Dec 2026
- WFP needs $13B in 2026; forecast to receive ~half
- FAO: fertilizer deficit → severe global food crisis within 6–12 months

## Bias Assessment
FAO/UN News/WEF are institutional sources with rigorous methodology; NPR mainstream. The causal attribution to Hormuz is supported by trade-flow data. "Crisis" framing is warranted by the famine confirmations, though humanitarian agencies have an incentive to highlight funding gaps.
