# 2026 Global Food Crisis — UNHCR/WFP Joint Report

## Summary
318 million people face crisis-level hunger or worse globally in 2026. 85M+ forcibly displaced from food-crisis contexts. Humanitarian food funding collapsed 59% since 2022. Two simultaneous famines confirmed in Gaza and Sudan. Hormuz closure now directly disrupting global agricultural supply chains via fertilizer shortage.

## Key Data Points
- 318 million facing crisis hunger or worse (2026)
- Crisis doubling: 105M in 2016 → 266M in 2025 → 318M projected 2026
- Conflict-driven hunger: doubled from 74M (2018) to 140M (2025)
- Weather-driven: tripled from 29M to 96M
- Economic-shock driven: 6x increase from 10M to 59M
- Displaced: 85M+ forcibly displaced across food-crisis contexts (2025)
- Active famines: Gaza and Sudan (first simultaneous famines this century, confirmed 2025)
- Famine risk 2026: Gaza Strip, Sudan, South Sudan
- Funding collapse: humanitarian food funding -59% from 2022-2025
- Hormuz disruption: directly affecting global fertilizer supply chains

UN agencies (UNHCR, WFP, FAO) are authoritative multilateral bodies with established methodologies. Possible mild pro-aid framing; data is well-sourced and peer-reviewed.
