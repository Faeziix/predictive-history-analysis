# How the Son of Iran's Last Shah Became a Pro-Israel Stooge

## Summary
A Middle East Eye **opinion** piece arguing that Reza Pahlavi has rebranded as a "born-again Zionist" positioning to seize power in any post-Islamic-Republic vacuum, and that this pro-Israel alignment discredits rather than legitimizes him as an opposition figure. It cites the Haaretz / TheMarker / Citizen Lab investigations as evidence that Israeli *hasbara* is "manufacturing support" for Pahlavi. Secondary, analytical context for [[reza-pahlavi-position-shift]] and [[israeli-influence-operation-iran]].

## Key Data Points
- Frames Pahlavi (eldest son of Mohammad Reza Pahlavi; crown prince of the 1926–1979 dynasty) as an "outdated hack" falsely projecting himself as leader of the current uprising.
- The Netanyahu–Pahlavi photo likened to **Massoud Rajavi (MEK) beside Saddam Hussein** during the Iran–Iraq war — the canonical traitor image in Iranian nationalist terms.
- Cites **Haaretz, TheMarker, and Citizen Lab** for the claim that Israeli hasbara manufactures Pahlavi's support.

## Bias Assessment
This is **opinion/advocacy**, not reporting — explicitly anti-Pahlavi and anti-Israel in framing, so its *characterizations* carry low evidentiary weight. Its value here is (1) it independently routes back to the same primary forensic sources ([[2025-10-02-citizenlab-prisonbreak-report]]), and (2) it documents the Iranian-nationalist *reception* of Pahlavi's pro-Israel turn — relevant to whether the influence op's "manufactured support" actually converts to organic legitimacy. Treat the factual core (the investigations exist) as solid; treat the editorializing as the author's view.
