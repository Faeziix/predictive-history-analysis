# Trump's China State Visit (May 13–15); Taiwan as "Negotiating Chip"; Stablecoin–Treasury Mechanism Live

## Summary
Trump made a state visit to China on May 13–15, 2026 — the first US presidential visit in ~9 years, lending surface plausibility to the "Nixon-1972-scale reset" framing. Xi placed Taiwan at the center, calling it "the most important issue" and warning differences could lead to a clash. Trump declined to commit to a $14B Taiwan arms sale, calling it a "very good negotiating chip." Separately, the GENIUS Act (signed July 2025) operationalizes the predicted financial mechanism: USD-pegged stablecoins backed by Treasuries to create a new demand pool for US debt as China/Japan reduce purchases.

## Key Data Points
- State visit May 13–15, 2026; first US president in China in ~9 years; Xi invited to US Sept 24, 2026
- Taiwan central: Xi "most important issue"; Trump declined to confirm US defense of Taiwan; $14B arms sale held as "negotiating chip"
- Summit ended with "strategic stability" framing but no substantive agreements announced
- GENIUS Act (July 2025): first US federal framework for payment stablecoins
- USD stablecoins = 98% of global stablecoin market; ~$272.9B market cap; $36.3T annual on-chain volume
- Explicit US aim: stablecoin reserves → new Treasury demand to defend dollar reserve status as China/Japan retreat

## Bias Assessment
WaPo, CNN, CSIS, CFR, Atlantic Council are mainstream/institutional with high reliability. The "grand bargain" interpretation (linking the visit + Taiwan + stablecoins into one design) is analytical; the component facts (visit, Taiwan-as-chip, GENIUS Act mechanism) are independently documented. No comprehensive bargain has been concluded.
