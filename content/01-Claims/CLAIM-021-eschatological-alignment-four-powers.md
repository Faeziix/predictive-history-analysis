# CLAIM-021: Four Powers Share Eschatological Alignment

## The Claim

Despite appearing to oppose each other, all four are "working together" toward the same eschatological outcomes: the building of the Third Temple, the War of Gog and Magog, the rise of the Antichrist, and ultimately the end-of-age transition. This shared script explains why the war is escalating despite ostensibly opposing interests.

The Jews' return to Israel, the Third Temple, the Messiah/Antichrist cycles have repeated throughout history (Cyrus the Great, Roman-Jewish wars, rise of Islam, Crusades) and the current era is another iteration. All four nations' religious traditions encode this same script because they all trace back to the same Levantine source.
