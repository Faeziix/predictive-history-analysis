# CLAIM-020: AI Will Replace Money as the New "God" System

## The Claim

The current era represents a transition from money as the primary social control mechanism ("money as God") to AI as the new control system ("AI God"). For AI to become the new god it requires: (1) omniscience — combining all world databases (health, school, work, police, banking, browsing history) into one surveillance system, and (2) religion — making people believe AI is conscious, divine, or spiritually animated. The endpoint is techno-slavery: a microchip or AI system that controls all human attention and behavior while people believe it is helping them live their best life.

## Framework Context

Professor Jiang (GT-28) frames this as the deepest layer of current geopolitical conflict. The "civil war" between transnational capital (capitalism, freedom of money) and the AI-god faction (techno-Marxism) explains why nationalism, religion, and AI are simultaneously rising as forces against the existing system. Trump's Technate ([[CLAIM-008-trump-technate]]) is the transition management structure.

## Causal Chain

1. Money = alchemy (lead into gold through belief/imagination)
2. Money functions because it is omnipresent and everyone believes in it
3. Money's weakness: must print → must destroy → creates instability
4. AI = new alchemy: turns data into power through omniscience
5. AI needs database consolidation + religious legitimacy to replace money
6. Civil war: transnational capital resists this transition (loss of freedom)
7. Nationalist/religious/tech factions push toward AI-god system
8. Endpoint: techno-slavery — AI controls human attention as the new "energy capture" mechanism

## Status: EVOLVING

Observable evidence of database consolidation (social credit in China, surveillance capitalism in West), AI religious narratives (OpenAI's AGI religion, "summoning demons" framing from Sam Altman), and AI investment concentration supports the trajectory. The "civil war" framing is consistent with tensions between nationalist movements and globalist finance.
