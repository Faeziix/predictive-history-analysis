# Putin Will Remain in Power for Another 20 Years

## Original Claim

> Professor Jiang predicts Vladimir Putin will live and remain in power for approximately another 20 years from 2026 — placing any succession crisis in the 2040s.

## Context
Stated in the Final Exam Q&A when a student asked what happens if Putin ceases to be leader. Jiang's prediction is based on his read of Putin's health, political control, and historical pattern of strongman leaders. The prediction matters because it determines when the post-Putin expansion phase ([[russia-post-great-leader-expansion]]) becomes relevant.

## Evidence For
- [[2026-05-28-russia-post-putin-expansion]] — Primary source; Jiang's direct statement: "I think Putin is going to live a lot longer than people believe. I think he's going to be around for another 20 years."

## Evidence Against
- Western intelligence assessments have periodically suggested Putin may have health issues (Parkinson's disease rumors, cancer rumors) — none confirmed.
- Age: Putin was born 1952; 20 more years from 2026 = ruling until age ~94 (historically unprecedented for a sitting head of state).
