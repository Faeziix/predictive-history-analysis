# Trump Third Term and Political Continuity of War Agenda

## Original Claim

> For the American imperial plan to succeed, it requires something Jiang calls "continuity of agenda" — Trump must secure a third term (or equivalent authoritarian continuity beyond 2028) because the plan is too long to survive a change of government. Without continuity, the entire Technate-building strategy collapses if Democrats win in 2028. Trump's method: electoral fraud, cheating, rigging elections.

## Context

Introduced in Game Theory #21 as the second of three requirements for America's imperial strategy to succeed (the other two being a national draft and an AI surveillance state). Jiang argues the 10-20 year timeline for containing China via choke-point control cannot tolerate a presidential transition in 2028 — Democrats would either continue the plan (having no alternative) or disrupt it. Trump's survival strategy is not democratic — it is electoral manipulation.

The claim is notable for how Jiang frames Democratic complicity: even if Democrats win, they have no alternative plan and would likely continue Trump's strategy. But Trump himself won't risk the uncertainty — hence third term.

## Evidence For

- No formal evidence yet. Claim is forward-looking (2028 election horizon).
- Context: growing normalization of Trump challenging electoral norms (January 6, ongoing election fraud claims)
- Democratic complicity in war vote (Congress war powers resolution engineered to fail) suggests bipartisan alignment, making peaceful transition less disruptive than Jiang implies
- Historical precedent: FDR won four terms during WWII; wartime presidents have sought continuity before
- 22nd Amendment limits Trump to two terms unless repealed or circumvented
- [[SRC-033]] (Game Theory #21) — primary source for this claim

## Evidence Against

- 22nd Amendment is a substantial legal barrier; circumventing it would require Supreme Court ruling or constitutional amendment
- Jiang acknowledges "he'll cheat" as the mechanism — not institutional route
- High domestic polarization makes election manipulation at scale difficult to conceal
- Democratic complicity in war suggests the plan might survive without Trump personally
