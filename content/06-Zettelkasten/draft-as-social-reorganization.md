# Draft as Social Reorganization

**Core idea:** The purpose of a national draft is not primarily military manpower — it is social engineering to reintegrate idle young men into the productive economy. The military context is secondary; the social restructuring is primary.

## The Problem Being Solved

Modern Western societies have produced a generation of young men who are:
- Gambling online, watching pornography (OnlyFans), buying Bitcoin
- Attending college for four years without learning useful skills
- Unwilling to work because wages are too low relative to digital entertainment
- Not contributing effectively to the economy

This is *structurally useful* for elites during stable periods (compliant consumers), but becomes a liability during economic decline and war mobilization.

## The Solution

1. **Engineer a financial crisis** (like the Great Depression) → Parents lose money → Young men are forced to work or starve.
2. **Deploy a national draft** → Government trains young men in practical skills (engineering, logistics, military trades) — often for free, better than universities.
3. **Result:** Young men become skilled, disciplined contributors to the war economy/rebuilding economy.

> "A draft doesn't necessarily mean that you go to war. A draft just means that you work for the government for like 5 to 10 years. Unlike schools, the military is incentivized to actually teach you something useful."

## International Scope

Jiang explicitly predicts the draft will come not just to the US but to **Canada, Australia, and Europe** — indicating this is a civilizational restructuring of the Western world, not just American policy.

## Survival Logic

> "A natural draft means that if you're useful, they'll keep you alive. If you're not useful, they'll send you to Iraq where you'll die."

The draft functions as brutal meritocracy: demonstrate useful skills or be expended in combat.
