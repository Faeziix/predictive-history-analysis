# Reza Pahlavi — The Position Shift

**Core idea:** The exiled crown prince's stance on foreign military intervention did not shift once but repeatedly — from "red line" anti-intervention, to explicit denial that he was calling for it, to cheering a US strike as "humanitarian intervention," to later disclaiming that he was ever pro-war. The trajectory tracks the [[casualty-inflation-narrative|casualty narrative]] closely.

## The Four Stages

1. **April 2024 — "red line."** Pahlavi called military action against Iran [a "red line."](https://thearabweekly.com/reza-pahlavi-calls-us-military-intervention-iran-seeks-lead-transition)
2. **January 2026 — explicit denial.** In an Iran International interview, asked directly about foreign military action, he said: [**"I am not calling for any foreign military attack or intervention… the natural army in place are the people of Iran."**](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog_entry/irans-reza-pahlavi-calls-for-international-intervention-in-protests-islamic-republic-will-fall/)
3. **February 2026 — the reversal.** He told Reuters that [US military intervention "could save lives,"](https://www.jpost.com/international/article-886609) and after the strike (28 February) called it a [**"humanitarian intervention"**](https://thehill.com/policy/international/5739462-trump-regime-change-iran/) whose target was "the Islamic Republic… not the great nation of Iran," urging Iranians to [**"finish the job in this final battle."**](https://www.foxnews.com/world/exiled-crown-prince-calls-on-iranian-people-finish-job-cheers-trump-humanitarian-intervention)
4. **Afterward — denial again.** He has since distanced himself, disclaiming that he was ever pro-war.

Each stage is on the record. The reversal to pro-intervention coincides with the peak of the [[casualty-inflation-narrative|36,500 casualty narrative]] — the emotional climate in which intervention could be reframed as humanitarian.

## Connection to the Influence Operation

Pahlavi is also the figure the [[israeli-influence-operation-iran|documented Israeli influence operation]] had been promoting since before the protests — fake personas and AI avatars boosting his image and the monarchist "Javid Shah" frame. His rise and his usefulness to a foreign-intervention case were, to a documented degree, manufactured rather than purely organic.
