# HRANA Methodology — The Verified Counter-Figure (~7,000)

**Core idea:** The US-based Human Rights Activists News Agency produced a conservative, name-by-name verified death toll of ~7,000 — roughly one-fifth of the [[casualty-inflation-narrative|Iran International figure]] — using a documented "minimum verifiable cases" method with a real track record of accuracy.

## The Figure

HRANA's confirmed toll rose from [**6,126 (27 January 2026)**](https://www.npr.org/2026/01/27/nx-s1-5689793/6-126-iran-crackdown-protests-death-toll) to roughly **7,007–7,015 (early–mid February)**, with another ~11,744 cases still *under review* and deliberately excluded from the confirmed count. The full [50-day "Crimson Winter" report](https://www.en-hrana.org/the-crimson-winter-a-50-day-record-of-irans-2025-2026-nationwide-protests/) breaks the confirmed total into protesters, children, non-protester civilians, and military/government dead.

## The Method

HRANA documents [**"minimum verifiable cases rather than maximal estimates"**](https://www.en-hrana.org/the-crimson-winter-a-50-day-record-of-irans-2025-2026-nationwide-protests/), built from **more than 143,330 reports** drawn from confidential and open sources, with each death cross-checked against a primary plus an additional confidential source and named victims paired with identifiers (age, location, documentation anchors). NPR notes HRANA [**"has been accurate in multiple rounds of unrest"**](https://www.npr.org/2026/01/27/nx-s1-5689793/6-126-iran-crackdown-protests-death-toll) and verifies each death through an on-the-ground network. In 2022, its count matched Iran's *own* interior-ministry figures — a track record the higher number does not have.

The method is self-described and not externally audited; [Al Jazeera's profile of HRANA](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/1/15/what-is-hrana-the-us-based-group-behind-irans-death-toll-figures) notes the group declined to disclose corroborating contacts, citing security. But no source refutes it, and skeptical outlets treat HRANA as the *more* rigorous source relative to Iran International.

## The Honest Ceiling

> **Discipline note:** Because of the [[iran-internet-blackout|blackout]], even HRANA's ~7,000 is **not independently confirmed** — it is the most rigorous *reported* count, not a proven one. "Rigorous, named, methodologically conservative" and "anonymous, unpublished, by-the-outlet's-own-admission-unverifiable" are not the same tier of evidence. And ~7,000 is still an enormous number of people who should never have died — responsibility for that crackdown rests squarely with the [[iran-actor|Islamic Republic]].
