# The Israeli Influence Operation in Iran

**Core idea:** Months *before* the December protests, a joint Haaretz / TheMarker investigation — corroborated by the University of Toronto's Citizen Lab — documented an Israeli-funded, AI-driven network of fake Iranian personas amplifying pro-[[reza-pahlavi-position-shift|Pahlavi]], pro-monarchy content in synchrony with Israeli military action. This is the part of the "foreign hand" question that is *forensically documented* rather than merely alleged by the regime.

## The Investigation

Two complementary investigations underpin this: the **primary forensic source** is [[2025-10-02-citizenlab-prisonbreak-report|Citizen Lab Report No. 189 — "PRISONBREAK" (2 October 2025)]], and the human-sourced corroboration is the [joint Haaretz / TheMarker investigation (3 October 2025)](https://theiranpost.com/the-israeli-influence-operation-in-iran-pushing-to-reinstate-the-shah-monarchy/). Together they documented an **Israeli-funded, Persian-language influence operation** using **fake personas and AI-generated "avatars" posing as Iranian citizens** on X and Instagram to **incite revolt, boost Reza Pahlavi, and promote restoring the monarchy**. Citizen Lab assessed it **most likely run by an Israeli government entity or a close contractor** (high likelihood), while explicitly noting it "cannot conclusively attribute this to a particular entity."

### The forensic indicators (PRISONBREAK)

This is the part that is *research*, not allegation — coordinated-inauthentic-behavior signals on **50+ X accounts** (53 analyzed):

- **Dormant-then-activated:** accounts created Feb–Dec 2023, but **~99.5% of posting after January 2025** — a network held in reserve, then switched on.
- **Timezone fingerprint:** posting ramped at 6:00 UTC, peaked 7:00 UTC, fell off after 15:00 UTC — a work day in **UTC+3 (Israel)**, not organic global behavior.
- **Client fingerprint:** ≥75% of posts via X's **desktop web app** (anomalous for a mobile-first platform).
- **Shared infrastructure:** ~half registered via the same obscure "b…" email domain; identical hashtag strings; stolen/obscured profile photos (30 of 53); coordination with the `@TelAviv_Tehran` persona.

### Synchronized with Israeli strikes

- **13 June 2025** (12-day war opens): ≥9 near-identical posts of alleged explosions / unrest the same day.
- **23 June 2025 — Evin Prison:** an AI **deepfake** of the bombing posted at 12:05 Tehran time **while airstrikes were still ongoing**, with calls to storm the prison within ~90 minutes — timing that "strongly suggests prior knowledge of the Israeli military action." Israeli FM **Gideon Saar reposted** it.
- **Co-opted authentic protest hashtags** ("Death to Khamenei," "8 o'clock cry") to amplify inauthentic accounts.

### AI-generated propaganda catalogued

Fake **BBC Persian** articles (BBC confirmed it never published them); a deepfake of musicians **Toomaj Salehi, Mehdi Yarrahi, Shervin Hajipour** singing altered "Baraye" lyrics (visible AI artifacts); water-crisis propaganda; and the [Netanyahu–Pahlavi "Free Tehran" video](https://theiranpost.com/the-israeli-influence-operation-in-iran-pushing-to-reinstate-the-shah-monarchy/).

## The Mossad Influence Branch

Israel later confirmed the institutional capacity directly. The head of Mossad's Influence Operations Branch — established in 2021 under director David Barnea — told [Israel Hayom](https://www.israelhayom.com/2026/05/28/secret-mossad-branch-revealed-we-are-not-done-with-iran-we-are-just-getting-started/) that *"carrying out an influence operation that ultimately gets rid of someone is much cheaper and simpler than an assassination operation"* — the [[law-of-asymmetry|asymmetric logic]] of substituting narrative for force.

## What It Does and Does Not Prove

> **Discipline note:** This investigation proves an **inauthentic amplification operation promoting Pahlavi and the monarchy**. It does **not**, by itself, prove that the [[casualty-inflation-narrative|36,500 casualty figure]] was planted by that operation. Those are two distinct claims and must stay distinct. What it *does* establish: the information environment in which the casualty narrative went viral was **not neutral** — a known, forensically-documented actor was already operating in exactly those channels before December 2025.
