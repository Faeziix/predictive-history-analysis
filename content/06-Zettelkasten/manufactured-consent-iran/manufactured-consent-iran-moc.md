# Manufacturing Consent — The 2025–2026 Iran Casualty Narrative

The 2025–2026 Iran protests began as an economic uprising and ended folded into a case for war. These notes trace the *information-warfare* layer that Professor Jiang's framework predicts but that the [[CLAIM-001-us-iran-war|US–Iran war]] claim file does not, on its own, capture: how a casualty figure that was — by its own publisher's admission — impossible to verify came to dominate the narrative, how it ran roughly five times above the most rigorous independent count, and how it converged with a [forensically documented](https://theiranpost.com/the-israeli-influence-operation-in-iran-pushing-to-reinstate-the-shah-monarchy/) Israeli AI influence operation promoting [[reza-pahlavi-position-shift|Reza Pahlavi]].

The thesis of this cluster is narrow and disciplined: not that any single number was *proven a deliberate lie*, but that the information environment in which consent for war was built was demonstrably non-neutral, and that the mechanism mirrors a known precedent — the unverified [[october-7-al-aqsa-flood|October 7]] "40 beheaded babies" claim.
