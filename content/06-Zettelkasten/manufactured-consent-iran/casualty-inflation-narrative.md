# The Casualty Inflation Narrative (Iran International, 36,500)

**Core idea:** The dramatic death toll that went viral and became the war-justifying narrative — escalating from 12,000 to 36,500 over about ten days — was self-sourced to anonymous regime insiders and to documents the outlet says it reviewed but never published, and was by the outlet's own admission impossible to verify.

## The Escalation

[Iran International](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202601130145) reported **"at least 12,000 killed"** on 13 January 2026. By 25 January that had become [**"over 36,500 killed"**](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202601255198), framed as the deadliest two-day massacre in history and tied to the 8–9 January crackdown. The figure climbed in stages — 12,000, then 27,500, 30,000, 33,000, 36,500 — across roughly ten days.

## The Sourcing Problem

By the outlet's own account, the figure rested on **anonymous insiders** — "a source close to the Supreme National Security Council; two sources in the presidential office; accounts from several sources within the IRGC" — plus eyewitnesses, medical staff, and [classified documents the outlet says it reviewed but never published](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202601255198). Iran International conceded the obvious: *"under a communications blockade and without direct access to information, confirming a final figure will require further, detailed documentation,"* and that *"independent verification remains impossible."*

The figure is roughly **five times** the [[hrana-methodology|name-by-name verified count]] of ~7,000. The gap is the story.

## What This Note Does and Does Not Claim

> **Discipline note (carried from the source article):** This note does **not** claim Iran International knowingly fabricated a number. Proving deliberate deception would require showing the outlet *knew* the figure was false, and that evidence does not exist. What the record supports is narrower and still serious: the figure was **self-sourced**, **by the outlet's own admission unverifiable**, ran **~5× above** the most rigorous independent count, and nonetheless became the **dominant, viral, war-justifying narrative**. "Unverified and inflated" is provable; "deliberate lie" is not.

This is the [[manufacturing-consent-mechanism|consent-manufacturing mechanism]] in operation — and at least one major outlet named it as such at the time, in an Al Jazeera opinion piece titled ["Appropriating the death count: manufacturing consent for an attack on Iran."](https://www.aljazeera.com/opinions/2026/1/31/appropriating-the-death-count-manufacturing-consent-for-an-attack-on-iran)
