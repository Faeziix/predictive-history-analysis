# Pax Judaica vs. Pax Silica

**The debate:** Is the emerging world order shaped by Israel (Pax Judaica) or by AI/silicon-backed tech networks (Pax Silica)?

## Jiang's Answer: Pax Silica Requires Pax Judaica

Pax Silica is a **trade network**. Trade networks are:
- Fragile (dependent on multi-nation cooperation)
- Historically always subordinate to nation-state power
- Unable to enforce themselves without military backing

> "Power comes from the nation state. Unless you have a nation state backing Paxilica, it's not going to work."

Therefore: Whatever nation-state backs the AI/silicon trade network *is* the real power. Jiang's argument is that Israel is positioned to be that backer — giving it control from behind the AI order.

> "Behind Paxilica will be Pax Judaica. That's a possibility. But then Pax Judaica is in charge. Not Paxilica."

## Why Israel?

- CENTCOM transfer to Israel post-US-Iran war collapse ([[CLAIM-001-us-iran-war]])
- Israel's AI surveillance export capability ([[CLAIM-007-pax-judaica]])
- Israel's nuclear deterrent as the last standing military force in the region
- Historical control of intelligence networks ([[CLAIM-013-epstein-intel-networks]])

## The Framework Implication

"Pax Silica" advocates (tech accelerationists, Andreessen, etc.) are describing the *surface layer* of the new order — the visible trade/tech network. Jiang claims the real power sits underneath it: the nation-state that controls military force and intelligence.

This is consistent with Jiang's general framework: **always look for the nation-state backer behind the trade/financial network.**

## Historical Analogues

- British East India Company (trade network) backed by Royal Navy (nation-state)
- Federal Reserve (financial network) backed by US military (nation-state)
- Pax Silica (AI/tech trade) → backed by Israel (Pax Judaica)
