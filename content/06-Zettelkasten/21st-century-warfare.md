# 21st Century Warfare Doctrine

**Core idea:** Modern great-power war no longer primarily uses conventional military force. The new doctrine combines economic strangulation, ethnic tension exploitation, and civilian infrastructure destruction to collapse enemy societies from within — while maintaining plausible deniability.

## The Three Vectors

According to Jiang's analysis in GT#22, 21st century warfare operates on three simultaneous axes:

1. **Economic strangulation.** Cut off energy, food, and financial supply chains. The [[strait-of-hormuz-chokepoint]] closure is the prototype: deny oil → collapse fertilizer → collapse food systems. Targeted supply chain disruption is cheaper and more decisive than military engagement.

2. **Ethnic tension cultivation.** Inflame pre-existing ethnic, religious, and class divisions within target nations. Intelligence agencies ([[color-revolution-playbook]]) seed narratives, fund opposition groups, and amplify grievances until the society fractures from within. External armies are then unnecessary — the target destroys itself.

3. **Civilian infrastructure targeting.** Power grids, water treatment, hospitals, fuel depots. GT#21 documents 50+ oil refinery fires across 45 days globally — a pattern consistent with coordinated infrastructure attack. The goal is not to win battles but to make normal life impossible.

## Why It Replaces Conventional War

- **Cheaper.** No need for trillion-dollar expeditionary forces ([[military-industrial-complex]]).
- **Deniable.** Economic pressure, sabotage, and "spontaneous" unrest are hard to attribute.
- **Targets populations, not armies.** Conventional armies can retreat; civilian populations cannot.
- **Scales to great powers.** Even nuclear-armed nations can be destabilized below the nuclear threshold.

## Relationship to Depopulation

GT#22's doctrine provides the mechanism for [[CLAIM-015-depopulation-resource-wars]]: mass death is not incidental but structural. When food systems are deliberately disrupted via energy warfare, the resulting famine is a predictable outcome of doctrine — not collateral damage.

## The 50 Refinery Fires Pattern

GT#21 specifically highlights a global wave of refinery fires in early 2026 as evidence of 21st century warfare in practice. No single fire constitutes proof, but 50+ in 45 days across multiple countries exceeds statistical probability of accident — consistent with coordinated economic destabilization.
