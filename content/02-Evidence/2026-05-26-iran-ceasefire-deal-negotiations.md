# US-Iran April Ceasefire Holding Barely; MOU "Largely Negotiated" (Then Retracted)

## Summary
Nearly 3 months into the war (started Feb 28), an April 8 ceasefire is fragile and a peace MOU is being negotiated. Trump called it "largely negotiated" on May 23 then walked it back May 24. US struck Iran as recently as May 25. Iran has survived 3 months of US/Israeli air assault and is still negotiating from a position where it demands Lebanon coverage and Hormuz reopening be linked.

## Key Quotes
> "The ceasefire is on life support" — President Trump
> "A peace deal with Iran that would reopen the Strait of Hormuz is 'largely negotiated'" — Trump, May 23
> US military strikes Iran as Trump says negotiations move forward — NPR headline, May 25
