# New: Russia Post-Putin Expansion Pattern + Putin 20-Year Longevity Prediction

## Summary
Professor Jiang adds two new predictions: (1) Putin will live another 20 years, and (2) when Putin eventually dies, Russia will follow the historical pattern of great-leader succession — internal faction fighting resolves by expanding militarily outward into neighbors. The expansion exhausts itself and the empire decays.

## Key Quotes
> "I think Putin is going to live a lot longer than people believe. I think he's going to be around for another 20 years."

> "So you look at Genghis Khan and Muhammad when they die, there's a pattern that emerges which is there's at first internal friction. So the different interests in Russia get together and they start fighting over succession. And then they decide we cannot possibly agree on succession. So what we'll do is we will just go to war with everyone."

> "Russia will just expand outwards. You have this massive expansion that might go on for 10-20 years when it basically conquers everyone around them because no one is as militaristic as they are."

> "Then what happens — just exhaustion — where they either lose or they win but everyone's exhausted and then they decay imperially."

> "What Russia is going to do is move towards a total war economy and completely focus on the conquest of its neighbors especially Ukraine but also the Baltic states as well and then central Asia."
