# Iran International Reports 36,500 Killed — Self-Sourced, Unverifiable

## Summary
Iran International escalated its protest death-toll reporting from "at least 12,000 killed" (13 January 2026) to "over 36,500 killed" (25 January 2026), framing it as the deadliest two-day massacre in history. The figure was self-sourced to anonymous Iranian security-institution insiders and to classified documents the outlet says it reviewed but never published, during a nationwide internet blackout. The outlet itself conceded the count could not be independently verified. This evidence bears on CLAIM-001 (the war's onset and the consent built for it) and CLAIM-008 (Trump's Middle East war), but as an *information-warfare* artifact rather than a confirmed casualty fact. See [[casualty-inflation-narrative]].

## Key Quotes
> "Over 36,500 killed in Iran's deadliest massacre, documents reveal" (Iran International, 25 Jan 2026)
> "under a communications blockade and without direct access to information, confirming a final figure will require further, detailed documentation"
> Sourced to "a source close to the Supreme National Security Council; two sources in the presidential office; accounts from several sources within the IRGC"

## Source Credibility Assessment
Iran International is an established Persian-language outlet but is openly oppositional and, here, the figure is self-sourced to anonymous regime insiders and to documents that were never published — making it the weakest-supported number in the corpus. It contradicts the [[hrana-methodology|HRANA name-by-name count]] (~7,000) by roughly 5×. Treat as a *claim*, not a verified toll.
