# Food/Fertilizer Crisis Validates the Oil→Fertilizer→Famine Cascade

## Summary
The predicted causal chain — Middle East conflict → Hormuz closure → fertilizer shortage → food crisis — is materializing in documented, quantified form. Hormuz carried ~30% of global fertilizer exports plus sulfur for phosphate production; with it closed, fertilizer prices rose ~80% YoY and the UN/FAO warns of a severe global food crisis within 6–12 months. 318 million people are in crisis-level hunger and Gaza and Sudan are in confirmed famine simultaneously — the first time this century. This is the strongest real-world validation of CLAIM-006's mechanism.

## Key Quotes
> "Roughly 35% of global crude oil exports, 20% of liquefied natural gas exports, and up to 30% of fertilizer exports transited through this narrow strip of water – along with sulfur, essential for phosphate fertilizer production."

> "Fertilizer prices are up roughly 80% year-on-year following the Strait of Hormuz crisis."

> "Two countries, Gaza and Sudan, are in confirmed famine simultaneously, the first time this century."
