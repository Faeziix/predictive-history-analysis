# US–Iran War Ends by Negotiated MOU, Not Failed Ground Invasion

## Summary
The 2026 US–Iran war is ending through a negotiated Memorandum of Understanding (signing 19 June, Geneva) after ~4 months of air/naval conflict — with **no US ground invasion**. This is the first major divergence between events and the predicted mechanism of CLAIM-001 ("US decisively loses via a failed ground invasion"). The general thesis that the US would not achieve decisive victory holds (Iran's regime survived, negotiating from demands), but the specific catastrophic ground-war mechanism was avoided in favor of mutual de-escalation.

## Key Quotes
> "On June 14, Pakistan's Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif announced that the United States and Iran had finalized a memorandum of understanding that would be signed on June 19."

> "The memorandum included the cessation of hostilities in Lebanon, an end to Iranian restrictions on the Strait of Hormuz and a reduction of U.S. military assets from the region, as well as some relief of sanctions on Iran and the release of some frozen Iranian assets."
