# Professor Confirms Draft Coming to Multiple Countries

## Summary
In the Final Exam Q&A, Professor Jiang gives his most direct and explicit confirmation of CLAIM-003, and expands its scope internationally. He also reframes the draft's *purpose* — not primarily as war mobilization, but as social reorganization of idle young men into economically productive contributors.

## Key Quotes
> "I do believe the draft is coming. But not only that, but I believe that the draft is coming to many countries including Canada, Australia, Europe."

> "The purpose of a draft is to reorganize society in a certain way... Young men go to college and for four years they drink beer and watch football games and they don't learn anything... So what you do is you engineer financial crisis like the great depression so that you're forced to work... and then you have an actual draft to teach you skills."

> "A draft doesn't necessarily mean that you go to war. A draft just means that you work for the government for like 5 to 10 years."

> "A natural draft means that if you're useful, they'll keep you alive. If you're not useful, they'll send you to Iraq where you'll die."
