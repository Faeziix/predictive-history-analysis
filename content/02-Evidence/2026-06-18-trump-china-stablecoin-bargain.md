# Trump–Xi Summit + GENIUS Act: Grand-Bargain Components Materialize, Package Unsealed

## Summary
Two of CLAIM-017's three components show real-world movement. (1) The diplomatic reset: Trump's May 13–15 China state visit — the first US presidential visit in ~9 years — gives the "Nixon-scale reset" framing surface plausibility, with Taiwan explicitly treated as a "negotiating chip." (2) The financial-repression mechanism: the GENIUS Act operationalizes USD-stablecoins backed by Treasuries to create new debt demand as China/Japan retreat from Treasuries. However, the summit ended with **no substantive agreements** — the comprehensive grand bargain is not concluded. This also bears on CLAIM-018 (Putin/dedollarization): the US stablecoin strategy is an active *counter-move* defending dollar reserve status.

## Key Quotes
> "Trump said a pending $14 billion deal to sell arms to Taiwan is a 'very good negotiating chip' and stated 'I haven't approved it yet.'"

> "The meetings ended without any substantive agreements announced on key issues."

> "By incorporating dollar stablecoins into compliant financial instruments, the United States hopes to consolidate the dollar's global reserve currency status and create a new demand pool for US treasuries."
