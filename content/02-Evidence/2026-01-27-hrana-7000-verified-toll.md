# HRANA Verified Toll ~7,000 — The Independent Counter-Figure

## Summary
The US-based Human Rights Activists News Agency (HRANA) reported a name-by-name verified protest death toll that rose from 6,126 (27 January 2026) to ~7,007–7,015 (early–mid February), with ~11,744 further cases excluded as still under review. HRANA uses a documented "minimum verifiable cases" method drawn from 143,330+ reports and has matched Iran's own interior-ministry figures in prior unrest. This is the most rigorous *reported* count — roughly one-fifth of the [[casualty-inflation-narrative|Iran International figure]] — though the internet blackout means even this is not *independently confirmed*. See [[hrana-methodology]].

## Key Quotes
> "The Associated Press has been unable to independently assess the death toll given authorities cutting off the internet and disrupting calls into the Islamic Republic."
> HRANA "has been accurate in multiple rounds of unrest" and verifies each death with a network of activists on the ground.
> 6,126 confirmed as of 27 Jan (5,777 protesters, 214 government forces, 86 children, 49 civilians); 41,800+ arrests.

## Source Credibility Assessment
NPR/AP is high-credibility; HRANA's method is self-described and not externally audited, but corroborated across NPR/AP, GOV.UK, Wikipedia, and others, and treated by skeptical outlets as the more rigorous source. Roughly 7,000 is still an enormous toll for which the Islamic Republic bears responsibility.
