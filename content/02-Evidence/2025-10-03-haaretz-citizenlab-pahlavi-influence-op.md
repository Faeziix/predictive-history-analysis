# Haaretz / Citizen Lab — Documented Israeli AI Influence Op Promoting Pahlavi

## Summary
The University of Toronto's **Citizen Lab Report No. 189 — "PRISONBREAK" (2 October 2025)** forensically documented a coordinated network of **50+ inauthentic X accounts** running an **AI-enabled influence operation** that incited Iranians to revolt and promoted Reza Pahlavi / the monarchy, assessed as **most likely run by an Israeli government entity or a close contractor** (high likelihood). The next-day [joint Haaretz / TheMarker investigation (3 October 2025)] added human sourcing (5 insiders; "private entity that receives government support"). The op ran dormant from 2023 and activated in January 2025, synchronized with the June 12-day war and the Evin Prison strike. Published *before* the December 2025 protests. Primary source: [[2025-10-02-citizenlab-prisonbreak-report]]. See [[israeli-influence-operation-iran]].

## Key Quotes
> "A coordinated network of more than 50 inauthentic X profiles is conducting an AI-enabled influence operation spreading narratives inciting Iranian audiences to revolt." — Citizen Lab, PRISONBREAK
> ~99.5% of the network's posting happened after January 2025, despite accounts being created in 2023; posting peaked in the UTC+3 (Israel) work-day window via X's desktop client.
> An AI deepfake of the Evin Prison bombing was posted at 12:05 Tehran time *while strikes were ongoing* — "strongly suggests prior knowledge of the Israeli military action." Israeli FM Gideon Saar reposted it.
> Citizen Lab assessed the op is most likely run by an Israeli government entity or contractor (high likelihood), while stating it "cannot conclusively attribute this to a particular entity."

## Source Credibility Assessment
The original is Haaretz/TheMarker (Israeli press, paywalled) with independent forensic corroboration from Citizen Lab — a serious, non-partisan research outfit — which gives the core finding high weight. Secondary coverage (DAWN, L'Orient Today) is consistent. Note the discipline line: this proves an amplification operation promoting Pahlavi; it does NOT prove the 36,500 casualty figure was planted by it.
