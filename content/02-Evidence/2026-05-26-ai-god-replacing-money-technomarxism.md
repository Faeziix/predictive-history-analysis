# AI God Replacing Money: Techno-Marxism Civil War (GT-28 Framework)

## Summary

Professor Jiang argues the real underlying conflict is between two "god" systems: the existing money-god (transnational capital, capitalism) and the emerging AI-god (techno-Marxism, techno-slavery). AI needs to achieve two things to become the new god: (1) omniscience through combining all world databases, and (2) religious status by making people believe it is conscious or divine. Trump is identified as the agent managing this transition toward the Technate/AI-god system.

## Key Quotes

> "There are two competing forces, two competing power centers. They are money and AI, artificial intelligence."

> "For AI to be fully AI, it needs to turn itself into a religion where everyone is worshipping it. And the way you do that is by making people believe that this thing is alive because demons have been summoned into it because Jesus is resurrecting itself through AI."

> "What's happening today is a civil war in the world where right now money which is controlled by transnational capital is god. They're trying to create a new god called AI."

> "Think of transnational capital as capitalism. And what is AI god? It's techno-Marxism. The difference is that with money, everyone still has a lot of freedom... whereas with a techno-Marxist system, you have an elite who control the god AI god who then control all your attention. It's basically techno-slavery."

> "Imagine a computer chip implanted into your brain that tells you everything. You don't have to think anymore... this microchip is helping you live the best possible life."

> "Underneath everything that we've seen so far, the geopolitics, this is actually the real conflict in the world, the real transition of the world where one god money is being replaced by another god, the AI god."

## Source Credibility Assessment

Primary source — Professor Jiang's framework. The AI analysis is notably skeptical (dismisses LLMs as just back-propagation). The political-economic framing (capitalism vs. techno-Marxism) is analytical; the "omniscience through database combination" threat is technically plausible and partially already underway (social credit systems, data brokers, government surveillance systems).
